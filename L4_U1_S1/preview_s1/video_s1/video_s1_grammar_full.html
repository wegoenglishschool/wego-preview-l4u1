A / AN
3 · Count nouns · singular

a or an?Singular = una cosa · a / an

a + consonant sound“a banana · a cookie”
an + vowel sound“an egg · an apple · an onion”
why an?“an apple” is easier to say than “a apple”
never aloneI want apple. → I want an apple.
-S -ES -IES
4 · Count nouns · plural

Two or more: add -sPlural = dos o más

+ segg → eggs · banana → bananas · cookie → cookies
+ es (o, ch)tomato → tomatoes · potato → potatoes · sandwich → sandwiches
y → iesstrawberry → strawberries
numberstwo eggs · three bananas · four cookies
SOME
5 · Non-count nouns

One form onlySin a / an · sin número · sin -s

💧 liquidswater · milk · juice · tea · soup
🧂 powders & grainssalt · rice · cereal
🧀 massesbread · cheese · meat · butter · chocolate
amount → somesome water · some rice · some bread
