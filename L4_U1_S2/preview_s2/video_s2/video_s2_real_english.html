Grab a bite
Meaning · SignificadoEat something quickly or have a fast meal.
Comer algo rápido.
When? · ¿Cuándo?You only have 10 minutes for lunch.
Example“Let's grab a bite before the movie starts.”
Pause & explain
⏸ PAUSE
Mouth-watering
Meaning · SignificadoFood that looks or smells so good it makes you want to eat it.
Se ve o huele delicioso.
When? · ¿Cuándo?You walk past a bakery and smell fresh bread.
Example“That pizza looks mouth-watering. I want a slice right now!”
Pause & explain
⏸ PAUSE
Dig in
Meaning · SignificadoStart eating enthusiastically.
Empezar a comer con ganas.
When? · ¿Cuándo?You cooked a big meal and it's finally on the table.
Example“The food looks amazing. Everyone, dig in!”
Pause & explain
⏸ PAUSE
Have a sweet tooth
Meaning · SignificadoLove eating sweets and desserts.
Ser muy de dulce.
When? · ¿Cuándo?Your friend always orders dessert at every restaurant.
Example“She has a sweet tooth. She eats chocolate every single day.”
Pause & explain
⏸ PAUSE
Pig out
Meaning · SignificadoEat a lot of food in an uncontrolled way.
Atascarse de comida.
When? · ¿Cuándo?You went to an all-you-can-eat buffet and ate way too much.
Example“We pigged out on pizza and ice cream all night.”
TipVery informal: use it with friends and family.
